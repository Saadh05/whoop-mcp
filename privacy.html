Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2025

This privacy policy describes how the Whoop MCP integration ("the App") handles your data. The App is a personal tool that connects your Whoop fitness data to Claude AI via the Model Context Protocol.

Data We Access

With your explicit authorization, the App accesses the following data from your Whoop account:

  • Profile information (name, email)
  • Body measurements (height, weight, max heart rate)
  • Recovery scores (HRV, resting heart rate, sleep performance)
  • Sleep data (duration, efficiency, sleep stages)
  • Workout data (strain, calories, sport type, heart rate zones)
  • Physiological cycles (daily strain, energy expenditure)

How We Use Your Data

Your Whoop data is used solely to answer your personal queries through Claude AI. Data is retrieved on-demand when you ask a question and is not stored beyond your local device. No data is shared with third parties.

Data Storage

OAuth tokens used to access the Whoop API are stored locally on your device at ~/.whoop_tokens.json. No data is stored on external servers.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. Your fitness data is only passed to the Claude AI model you are actively using in your local session.

Your Rights

You can revoke the App's access to your Whoop account at any time from the Whoop Connected Apps settings page. Deleting ~/.whoop_tokens.json removes all locally stored credentials.
